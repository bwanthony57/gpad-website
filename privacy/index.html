This Privacy Policy describes how BAGPIPER, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information for Golfer's Pair-A-Dice.

1. Summary

Golfer's Pair-A-Dice is a casual dice-rolling golf game. The app lets you create player profiles, play rounds, earn in-game rewards, buy optional in-game items, and, if enabled, view ads.

We aim to collect as little personal information as practical. Some information may be collected by Apple, app stores, payment processors, purchase-entitlement providers, advertising partners, and other service providers used to operate the app.

2. Information You Provide or Create in the App

The app may store gameplay and profile information, such as:

  • player name or nickname you enter;
  • saved player profiles;
  • game progress, XP, levels, titles, inventory, coins, purchases, and ad reward status;
  • round history, scores, tournaments, boss match progress, and related gameplay state;
  • settings and preferences.

This data is generally stored locally on your device. If account, cloud sync, or server-based features are added, this policy will be updated to describe what information is collected and how it is stored.

Do not enter sensitive personal information as a player name or nickname.

3. Accounts and Cloud Features

The app may not include user accounts, cloud sync, public leaderboards, chat, or public sharing at launch.

If account features are added, we may collect or process account-related information such as a user ID, sign-in provider identifier, email address, purchase entitlement status, cloud save data, and support information needed to operate the account. This policy will be updated before those features are made available.

If public leaderboards or similar social features are added later, player names and scores may become publicly visible and additional moderation or reporting measures may apply.

4. Purchases

The app may offer optional in-app purchases, such as consumable coin packs or item bundles and a non-consumable Remove Forced Ads purchase. Purchases are processed by the applicable app store, Apple App Store or Google Play, as applicable. We do not receive your full payment card number from the app store.

We use RevenueCat to help manage in-app purchases and entitlements. RevenueCat may process purchase identifiers, app user IDs, entitlement status, transaction information, and related diagnostics needed to validate, grant, and restore eligible purchases.

We may store purchase-related game state, such as whether an item was purchased, whether forced ads have been removed, or whether in-game coins or items should be granted. Coins and items have no cash value and cannot be redeemed, sold, transferred, or exchanged for money or prizes.

5. Advertising and Rewarded Ads

The app may show ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads), including optional rewarded ads that may grant in-game currency or rewards after completion. For non-premium users, the app may also show forced or interstitial Practice round break ads.

If offered, Remove Forced Ads is intended to remove eligible forced Practice round break ads only. Optional rewarded ads may remain available because they are user-initiated.

Google AdMob and related advertising partners may collect or receive information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, app interactions, ad impressions, diagnostics, and usage data. The exact data collected depends on the ad network, your region, platform settings, and device configuration.

On iOS, if required by platform rules, the app may request App Tracking Transparency permission before certain tracking-related ad activity. On Android and other platforms, ad-related data practices may be subject to platform settings and applicable consent requirements where required by law. We do not claim a separate in-app consent system beyond what the platform or ad SDK may present.

6. Analytics and Diagnostics

The app may receive operational diagnostics from Apple, Google, RevenueCat, Google AdMob, or other service providers used to operate purchases, ads, builds, or app distribution.

If additional analytics or crash-reporting services are added, this section and applicable app store privacy disclosures will be updated before submission or release.

7. Support Communications

If you contact us for support, we may collect your email address, your message, and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to you, troubleshoot issues, and improve the app.

Please do not send sensitive information unless necessary.

8. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • operate and save app functionality;
  • provide gameplay, progression, inventory, and rewards;
  • process and restore purchases;
  • show ads and measure ad performance;
  • prevent abuse or fraud;
  • provide support;
  • diagnose crashes and technical issues;
  • improve game balance, features, and user experience;
  • comply with legal obligations and applicable app store requirements.

9. Sharing Information

We may share information with:

  • Apple, Google, and applicable app store services for purchases, downloads, reviews, app analytics, and platform operations;
  • RevenueCat for purchase and entitlement management;
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) and related advertising partners;
  • analytics and crash-reporting providers, if used;
  • service providers who help operate websites, email, support, hosting, or business systems;
  • authorities or other parties when required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security.

We do not sell personal information in the ordinary sense of exchanging it for money. Some advertising or analytics practices may be considered "sharing" or "tracking" under privacy laws or platform rules, depending on the services used and your settings.

10. Children

Golfer's Pair-A-Dice is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

The app is intended as a general-audience casual game.

11. Your Choices

You may be able to:

  • reset or delete local app data by deleting the app from your device;
  • manage in-app purchases through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, as applicable;
  • restore eligible non-consumable purchases, such as Remove Forced Ads, in the app where available;
  • manage ad and privacy settings through your device or platform settings, where available;
  • contact us to request help with support communications or privacy questions;
  • request account deletion from Account Settings in the app, if account features are available, or by contacting us.

Deleting the app may delete locally stored gameplay progress if cloud backup or sync is not available. Account deletion requests are processed after verification and are not automatic.

12. Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device unless deleted by you, the app, or your operating system. Purchase entitlement records, support emails, and business records may be retained as long as reasonably necessary for support, legal, accounting, fraud prevention, or operational purposes.

13. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no system is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your device, app store account, and account credentials secure.

14. International Users

If you use the app outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

15. Changes to This Policy
