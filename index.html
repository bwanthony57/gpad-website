Golfer's Pair-A-Dice

A dice-powered golf strategy game for iOS. Pick your club, roll the dice, and try to put together a decent round.

Coming soon to the App Store.

You work through practice rounds, tournaments, upgrades, and tougher matches as you go. Some holes go exactly as planned. Others absolutely do not.

The app may include optional ads and in-app purchases for virtual items.

Golfer's Pair-A-Dice is not a gambling game. There is no real-money betting, wagering, cash prizes, or redeemable rewards. The dice are just part of how shots play out.
