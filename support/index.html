Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. If a purchase did not unlock correctly, open the app and use Restore Purchases where available. Temporary planned location: Pro Shop > Restore Purchases until the User Account screen exists. Long-term planned location: User Account > Purchases > Restore Purchases.

For refunds, Apple requires refund requests to be handled through Apple’s purchase support system.

Ads

The app may show ads, including optional rewarded ads. Current recommended ad direction is AdMob without mediation for the first launch, subject to final testing and privacy review. Rewarded ad availability can vary by region, device, ad inventory, and network status.

The Remove Forced Ads purchase removes eligible forced Practice round break ads. Optional rewarded ads may remain available because they are user-initiated and grant rewards.

Saved Progress

The app currently stores gameplay progress locally on your device unless future accounts/cloud sync are added. Deleting the app may delete local progress. Public leaderboards, chat, public sharing, and user-generated content are not planned for the initial launch.
