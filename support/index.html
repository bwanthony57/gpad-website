Common questions

Is Golfer's Pair-A-Dice a gambling app?

No. Golfer's Pair-A-Dice is a casual golf-themed dice game for entertainment. Dice are used as a gameplay mechanic only. The app does not offer real-money gambling, betting, wagering, cash prizes, or redeemable rewards.

How do in-app purchases work?

Purchases are processed by the applicable app store, Apple App Store or Google Play. Available items may include consumable virtual currency and item bundles, and a one-time Remove Forced Ads purchase. Coins and items have no cash value and cannot be redeemed, sold, transferred, or exchanged for money or prizes. All billing and refunds are handled under the applicable app store's payment rules.

How do Restore Purchases and Remove Forced Ads work?

Restore Purchases applies to eligible non-consumable purchases, such as Remove Forced Ads. Consumable purchases, such as coin packs, generally are not restored after they are used. If Remove Forced Ads does not appear after purchase, open the app and use Restore Purchases where available. If it still does not unlock, email support@golferspairadice.com with your platform, device, app version, and a description of the purchase.

A purchase did not unlock. What should I do?

Open the app and use Restore Purchases where available. If the item still does not appear, email support@golferspairadice.com with your platform, device model, OS version, app version, and a description of the purchase.

How do I request a refund?

Refund requests must be submitted through the applicable app store. On iOS, use Apple's purchase support system. On Android, use Google Play's refund process. We cannot process app store refunds directly.

Why do I see ads in the app?

The app may show ads, including optional rewarded ads that grant in-game rewards after completion. For non-premium users, forced or interstitial ads may appear during Practice round breaks unless you purchase Remove Forced Ads.

What does Remove Forced Ads do?

If offered, the Remove Forced Ads purchase removes eligible forced Practice round break ads. Optional rewarded ads may remain available because they are user-initiated and grant in-game rewards.

I watched a rewarded ad but did not receive the reward. What should I do?

Rewards are not guaranteed if the ad fails, is interrupted, cannot be verified, or if your daily reward limit has been reached. Wait a moment and check whether the reward appears in your inventory or balance. If it is still missing, restart the app and try again later. If the issue continues, email support@golferspairadice.com with your platform, device, app version, and what reward you expected.

I bought Remove Forced Ads but still see forced ads. What should I do?

Remove Forced Ads removes eligible forced Practice round break ads only. Optional rewarded ads may still appear because they are user-initiated. If you are seeing forced Practice break ads after purchasing, use Restore Purchases in the app. If forced break ads continue, email support@golferspairadice.com with your platform, device, app version, and when the ad appeared.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Gameplay progress is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app, changing devices, or clearing app data may delete saved progress unless a backup or cloud sync feature is available.
